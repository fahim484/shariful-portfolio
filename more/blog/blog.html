# From a Small Village School to University: The Beginning of a Life-Changing Journey
There are certain phases in life that quietly change who we are forever. For me, entering university was one of those phases — probably the most important chapter of my life so far.

I came from a small, non-famous village school with nothing extraordinary except a heart full of dreams. I was never the most talented student in the room, nor was I someone who could easily talk to people. Being naturally introverted made everything even harder. But deep inside, I always carried one simple dream — to do something meaningful in life.

That dream became the starting point of my university journey.

## The First Shock of University Life

My first real challenge came during the first semester. After spending two years of college life without proper academic focus, university suddenly felt like a completely different world. Everything was new, fast, and overwhelming.

The classrooms were filled with incredibly talented students. Many of them were confident, skilled, and academically strong. Standing among them, I slowly started feeling small. Self-doubt began to grow inside me.

I often questioned myself:

"Can I really survive in engineering?"

At the same time, my introverted nature made it difficult to make friends or connect with people. While others easily built friendships and adapted to university life, I struggled silently within myself.

## The Turning Point

Then came one of the biggest turning points of my life.

I joined a university program called *Youth Leadership Training*. At first, I did not know how much this program would change me as a person.

The very first lesson of the training was simple yet powerful — breaking hesitation.

I realized that I was not alone. Many students there were just like me. They had fears, insecurities, and hesitation hidden behind their silence. Everyone came there hoping to overcome the barriers inside themselves.

One person who deeply influenced me during this journey was our trainer, Affan Bhai.

He told us something that I still remember today:

> “Express your thoughts, even if they are wrong. Start conversations yourself. Maybe the other person is also waiting for you to speak first.”

Those words may sound simple, but they changed the way I looked at life.

## Learning Beyond Academics

University is not only about CGPA, assignments, or exams. Sometimes, the most valuable lessons come from experiences, people, and moments that help us grow internally.

Through that training program, I learned confidence, communication, and the courage to express myself. These lessons helped me transform my university life in ways I never expected.

Even today, I genuinely feel grateful to Affan Bhai for guiding us and helping students like me believe in ourselves.

## Final Thoughts

This is still just the beginning of my journey. I know there are many challenges ahead, many things left to learn, and many dreams left to achieve.

But when I look back at that quiet village boy who entered university full of fear and uncertainty, I realize something important:

Growth does not happen overnight. Sometimes, it begins with a single step outside your comfort zone.

And maybe, that step can change your entire life.
